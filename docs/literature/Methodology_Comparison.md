# Methodology comparison — 15 essential papers

**Scope update:** the November 22 proposal now governs the project (verbatim copy: `Project_Proposal_Reference.txt`). The 15 rows below remain the **full-arc** essential set, not a claim that each method is implemented for the December 3 Version B grid. For Version B, use a pre-existing frozen selector (especially P1 *SparseVLM* or *CRISP*) and train only the 15-action `(k,R)` controller from observed grid outcomes. Instruction-aware connector and learned vision-encoder papers chiefly inform later Version C. Do not confuse full-information controller learning with GRPO or decoder LoRA.

**Reading convention:** page references are 1-based pages of the copied/source PDF, not conference page labels. “Frozen” describes the **reported training stage**, not the mere existence of a frozen reference model. “Adaptive K” means the total number of visual tokens *actually changes between examples*, not merely that the token identities change at a fixed K. Not all papers report comparable answer accuracy, FLOPs, and end-to-end latency; do not rank raw numbers across backbones/datasets.

## Critical comparability controls

1. **Match both mean and distribution of K**, not just a nominal pruning ratio; StepPrune and MVP demonstrate that thresholding need not yield a fair operating point (StepPrune Fig.3, PDF pp.8–10; MVP Appendix Table 6, pp.13–14).
2. **Preserve trainability boundaries:** InternVL3.5 router-only ≠ its consistency-training stage; Glance scorer-only ≠ full self-distilled model; LLaVA-Mini alignment ≠ instruction tuning; TPRL frozen-LLM ablation ≠ headline model. See respective sections/pages above.
3. **Include selector, OCR/crop, teacher, and per-example value-fit overhead in cost**; post-ViT pruning does not erase the full vision-encoder cost. LoopZip Fig.8/Table 3 (PDF pp.7–8), Glance S15 (p.26), Hierarchical Query-Aware PDF p.5.
4. **Do not confuse three distinct “loops”:** recurrent weight-shared decoder calls (LoopZip/RecursiveVLM), training-time multiple pruning decisions with a one-shot deployed mask (TPRL), and decoder-layer progressive pruning (TOP-RL). See LoopZip §§3–4 (pp.2–5), RecursiveVLM §3 (pp.3–5), TPRL §3.5 (pp.7–8), TOP-RL methods (pp.3–5).
5. **The 15-paper P0 set is not a claim of complete field coverage.** `Research_Gap_Analysis.md` names material outside-corpus papers that change the novelty assessment.
