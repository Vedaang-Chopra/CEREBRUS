# Literature review and complete paper checklist

## Scope and provenance

All 77 original PDFs were text-extracted and checked in their method and experimental sections; 74 distinct PDF byte streams. Priority counts: P0 15, P1 12, P2 41, P3 9. Original files remain at their source paths; all 77 PDF copies are placed in priority folders. Page numbers below mean **1-based PDF pages**, not journal typeset page numbers. Each ID maps to one CSV row and one copied PDF (`ID__original-filename.pdf`).

The **primary project scope** is Vedaang Chopra’s *Adaptive Looped VLM — Research Vision & Implementation Blueprint* (November 22, 2026), supplied after the initial PDF review and preserved verbatim as `Project_Proposal_Reference.txt`. Its **Version B** is an existing text-guided selector plus a lightweight *pre-inference* controller choosing one of 15 `(k,R)` actions (`k` at 25/50/100% and `R` at 1–5) on `thinkingmachines/TML-d24-varloop-research`; the first experiment is a controlled grid and full-information cost-sensitive learning on its reward table. Version C (learned question-conditioned vision encoder), LoRA/GRPO, and safety-constrained training are gated later work, not the immediate method. The November 24 A40 loading/loop-control/ScreenSpot gate and December 3 course deliverable are **proposal targets, not verified achievements**. HTML/TXT/MD model cards in the frontier folder are contextual sources, **not PDF papers** and are not classified as additional studies. The supplied TML model card (release November 20, 2026) states shared middle-layer execution over 1–5 passes, a ~20 GB checkpoint and ≥32 GB memory for inference; these are provider statements, not a tested A40 deployment.

## Priority decision and caution

Read **P0 only first** (15 distinct PDFs); use the 12 P1 papers when selecting baselines/implementations. The 41 P2 and nine P3 files are retained only to satisfy full provenance, not recommended as input to a compact Claude session. The supplied literature does **not** establish a unique claim to question conditioning, adaptive visual-token counts, frozen-decoder training, RL token pruning, or looped-VLM compression in isolation. The proposal’s near-term contribution is a **controlled visual-budget × shared-decoder-loop grid on one checkpoint**, independently defined perception/reasoning probes, and a cheap controller trained on the *complete* 15-action reward table. It must be tested against the best fixed and non-learned allocations at matched realized cost. A trained loop-state-aware selector is an optional later hypothesis, **not Version B**. See `Research_Gap_Analysis.md` for external counterexamples.

#### [007] AdaptMerge: Inference Time Adaptive Visual and Language-Guided Token Merging for Efficient Large Multimodal Models (2025)

#### [013] Matryoshka Multimodal Models (2024)
**Source:** `02- Alignment/Matryoshka Multimodal Models.pdf` → `P0_Essential/013__Matryoshka Multimodal Models.pdf`. **Method and evidence:** Trains a single VLM to function at nested visual token scales; demonstrates an oracle gap and explicitly proposes an image/question-based scale predictor as future work. ## PDF integrity, duplicates and limitations

- Exact SHA-256-identical pairs: 030 ↔ 044 (Qwen2.5-VL), 040 ↔ 041 (TRM / *Less is More*), 075 ↔ 076 (both actually VITA-1.5). The second copy in each pair is P3 to avoid duplicate reading; both physical originals are copied and mapped in CSV. 77 PDFs therefore correspond to 74 distinct byte streams; textual near-duplicates were not exhaustively deduplicated.
- All 77 PDFs yielded selectable text by `pdftotext -layout`; no wholly unreadable or image-only PDFs found. This does not establish that every embedded figure is extractable; inspect figures in the source PDF when reproducing methods.
- The supplied TOP-RL PDF is nine pages and refers to additional appendix details not present in the supplied copy; training architecture/settings and claims dependent on that appendix remain incomplete (TOP-RL PDF pp.3–7).
- Source filenames are not authoritative bibliographic years: e.g. `2025_Patch_Ranking.pdf` prints a 2024 paper; `2025_LLaVA-PruMerge.pdf` and `2025_NVILA.pdf` print 2024 papers. The CSV records the paper year read from the PDF; ambiguous web publication histories are not inferred.
- Public code/weights claims are only credited where paper text or source model card documents them; availability is not installation verification. See `Research_Strategy.md` for a feasibility gate.
